Attribution & licenses

Map data

OpenStreetMap contributors. Road, building, natural-feature and street-furniture data are available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Data retrieved using the Overpass API and exported as GeoJSON. Overpass is the retrieval service; the underlying map data comes from OpenStreetMap. Local exports may include corrections and are transformed into procedural geometry for display.

Elevation

NASA SRTM (HGT tiles)

Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) elevation data from NASA/JPL, obtained through NASA Earthdata and the Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center (LP DAAC). Elevation tiles are sampled and converted into local terrain meshes.

See the NASA science data license policy, data-use and citation guidance, and the SRTM Global 1 Arc Second product record. Dataset-specific notices take precedence; Earthdata is a distribution portal, not a single license for every hosted dataset.

Monaco terrain (GeoTIFF)

Source: IGN, MNT LiDAR HD (Mod?le Num?rique de Terrain), obtained from the cartes.gouv.fr download service. Produced by the Institut national de l'information g?ographique et foresti?re (IGN). See the official dataset record.

Available under the Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0. Multiple source tiles were combined into the Monaco GeoTIFF; elevation samples are reprojected and resampled into Wander's local terrain mesh. The resulting in-game terrain is a derived representation, not an unmodified IGN product. The HGT fallback uses the NASA SRTM source credited above.
